DejaVu Sans is used here for the text the application draws on screen.

Panda3D's own font has no Cyrillic in it, so Russian and Kazakh came out as
rows of empty boxes - two of the three languages this application speaks.
DejaVu covers both, including the letters Kazakh needs that Russian does not
(ae, gh, q, ng, oe, u, ue, h, i with their own shapes).
